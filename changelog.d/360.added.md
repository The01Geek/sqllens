`agent.standing_instructions` (`SQLLENS_AGENT__STANDING_INSTRUCTIONS`): rules
for a deployment that SQL Lens adds to the assistant's instructions on every
request, after its built-in rules. An instruction that names the meaning of an
ambiguous term settles that choice ahead of memory notes and saved pairs, and
an instruction to ask about a choice keeps it open until the user picks a
meaning. Unset or blank text changes nothing; text over 10,000 characters fails
the configuration load. (#358)
