Under a per-role rule set, the assistant can now look up the schema. A `SELECT`
on `information_schema.TABLES`, `COLUMNS` or `KEY_COLUMN_USAGE` returns only the
selected rule set's tables, its listed columns and the keys among them, in the
connected database. Names, types and key details are filled in; every other
field, such as row counts, sizes, defaults, comments and constraint names, comes
back as `NULL`. Other catalog views, the `mysql`, `performance_schema` and `sys`
schemas, and `SHOW` statements are still refused. See
[rule sets](docs/external/rule-sets.md). (#379)
