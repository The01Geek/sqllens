An unrecognized key in the `[agent]` section of `sqllens.toml`, or an unknown
`SQLLENS_AGENT__*` environment variable, now fails the configuration load
instead of being silently ignored, so a misspelled setting such as
`standing_instruction` is caught by `sqllens validate` and `sqllens serve`.
**Action required:** remove any `[agent]` key or `SQLLENS_AGENT__*` variable
SQL Lens does not document. The retired `show_memory_details` and `show_sql`
keys are still accepted and ignored, with a warning in the log. (#358)
